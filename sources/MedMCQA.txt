TITLE: MedMCQA: A Large-scale Multi-Subject Multi-Choice Dataset for Medical QA
AUTHORS: Pal et al.; released 2022 (AIIMS Delhi / NEET-PG exam data)
VENUE: GitHub (medmcqa/medmcqa); ACL 2022

ABSTRACT:
MedMCQA is a large-scale multiple-choice QA dataset for medical education and entrance examination, containing 194,000 questions spanning 21 medical subjects drawn from Indian medical qualifying examinations (AIIMS/NEET-PG). Each question has four answer options and a single correct answer, covering pre-clinical, para-clinical, and clinical medicine. It is intended for benchmarking medical knowledge of language models.

KEY FACTS:
- Modality: text (multiple-choice questions)
- Scale: 194,000 questions across  ́21 subjects; ~10% held out as test
- Geography: India (Indian medical exam context)
- Access: GitHub / HuggingFace, MIT license
- Intended tasks: medical multiple-choice QA, knowledge evaluation
- License: MIT
- Source type: educational examination corpus
- Limitations: derived from entrance exams (not clinical practice); single-correct-answer format; no explicit reasoning-chain labels; India-centric curriculum.
