TITLE: eICU Collaborative Research Database, a freely available multi-center database for critical care research
AUTHORS: Pollard, T.J., Johnson, A.E.W., Raffa, J.D., Celi, L.A., Mark, R.G., Badawi, O.
VENUE: Scientific Data 5, 180178 (2018). DOI: 10.1038/sdata.2018.178

ABSTRACT:
The eICU Collaborative Research Database is a multi-center intensive care unit database with records from 200,859 ICU admissions from 139,367 unique patients at 208 hospitals in the United States between 2014 and 2015. The database includes vital signs, care plan documentation, medications, laboratory measurements, and severity of illness scores, curated to support collaborative multi-center research, external validation, and federated learning of clinical models. Data are distributed through PhysioNet under a credentialed data use agreement.

KEY FACTS (from abstract + data descriptor):
- Modality: Electronic Health Records (EHR) — ICU critical care data
- Scale: 200,859 ICU admissions / 139,367 unique patients / 208 hospitals
- Geography: United States (208 US hospitals, multi-center)
- Access: PhysioNet, credentialed DUA (research-only, non-commercial)
- Intended tasks: multi-center generalization, external validation, federated learning (classification)
