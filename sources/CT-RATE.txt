TITLE: CT-RATE: A Multi-organ CT with 2.7M Radiology Text Reports and Lung Nodule Masks
AUTHORS: Hamamci et al. (NYU Langone and Istanbul Medipol University); released 2023/2024
VENUE: HuggingFace (ibrahimhamamci/CT-RATE); arXiv 2308.01023

ABSTRACT:
CT-RATE provides 50,188 volumetric chest CT scans (25,692 unique scans) from 21,304 patients, each paired with a structured radiology report (2.7M impression statements). It includes multi-organ segmentation masks (CT-RATE-2) and a radiology report labeling pipeline. Reports were originally written in English via a Turkish-to-English translation pipeline at a single institution, and labels were extracted from free text with RadBERT, introducing possible label noise.

KEY FACTS:
- Modality: CT (volumetric) + free-text radiology reports (text)
- Scale: 50,188 volumes / 25,692 unique scans / 21,304 patients; 2.7M report impressions
- Geography: single center — Istanbul Medipol University, Turkey
- Access: HuggingFace, CC BY-NC-SA 4.0
- Intended tasks: multi-organ abnormality detection, report generation, vision-language modeling
- License: CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 (non-commercial)
- Source type: clinical-care (single hospital)
- Limitations: single-center; report labels auto-extracted (RadBERT, possible noise); English-translated reports; no external/site diversity.
