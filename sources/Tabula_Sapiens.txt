TITLE: The Tabula Sapiens: A molecular atlas of diverse cell types across the human body
AUTHORS: The Tabula Sapiens Consortium
VENUE: Science 376, 6594 (2022). DOI: 10.1126/science.abl4896

ABSTRACT:
The Tabula Sapiens is a framework for the human cell atlas that integrates single-cell transcriptomic data from 483,152 cells across 24 tissues and 15 donors, using a unified processing pipeline. The atlas provides cell-type annotations spanning many human organs and is intended to serve as a reference for tissue mapping, cell-type identification, and cross-tissue comparative studies. The dataset is released openly to accompany this publication as a community resource.

- Modality: single-cell transcriptomics (single-cell RNA-seq)
